Precision, recall and F1 per sample, caller, variant type (SNV, INDEL) and
GIAB v3.3 stratum. ``tp_base`` and ``fn`` count truth records, ``tp_call`` and
``fp`` count call records, as in the GA4GH benchmarking definitions. ``all`` is
every confident region inside the evaluation intervals. Strata with few truth
variants give unstable ratios; read the counts next to the ratios.
